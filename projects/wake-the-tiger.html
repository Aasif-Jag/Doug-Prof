Wake the Tiger

Art direction on Bristol's immersive amazement park — a built world of interconnected rooms that visitors move through rather than watch.

Scroll

Wake the Tiger is Bristol's permanent immersive amazement park — a multi-room built environment designed to disorient, delight and provoke. Doug was brought in as art director to shape the visual and experiential language of the attraction: from the architecture of individual rooms through to the coherence of the world as a whole.

The project demanded the same skills as large-scale festival work — coordinating multiple creative teams, holding a vision across months of build — but applied to a permanent venue with commercial pressures and repeat-visitor expectations. The clearest demonstration of Doug's consultancy at commercial scale.
