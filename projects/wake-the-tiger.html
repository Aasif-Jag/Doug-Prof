Wake the Tiger

Art direction on Bristol's immersive amazement park — a built world of interconnected rooms that visitors move through rather than watch. The clearest demonstration of Doug's consultancy at commercial scale.

Wake the Tiger opened in Bristol in 2022 as one of the UK's most ambitious permanent immersive attractions. Visitors move through a sequence of richly-detailed rooms, each with its own narrative, atmosphere and sensory character.

Doug's role spans art direction across the full visitor experience — ensuring visual coherence, narrative logic and the kind of detail that rewards repeated visits.
