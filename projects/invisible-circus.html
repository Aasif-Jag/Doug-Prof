Invisible Circus

Twenty-five years of radical theatre in disused and heritage buildings. The practice everything else grew out of.

Doug founded Invisible Circus in 2000 as a radical performance company specialising in site-specific work in disused and heritage buildings. Over twenty-five years, the company has created landmark productions across the UK and internationally.

The practice pioneered many of the techniques now common in immersive theatre — environmental storytelling, multi-location audience routing, and the integration of circus, cabaret and live performance in architectural space.
